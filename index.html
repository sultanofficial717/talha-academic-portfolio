Featured Research

News-based forecasting of Brazilian energy prices

DataInsight Lab, FAST-NUCES • Aug 2026 to present
Problem Can news text improve short-term electricity price forecasts?
What I did Filtered ~490K Portuguese articles down to ~30K using MinHash/LSH deduplication and topic filters. Translated text with NLLB, labeled topics with Gemini, and scored sentiment with FinBERT. Combined text features with PLD/CMO price data using 1024-d BGE embeddings, supervised PLS, and walk-forward validation.
Result R² 0.8755 for 12-hour forecasting, a 5.0 percentage-point gain over market-only features, checked with Diebold-Mariano and bootstrap tests.
What I learned Without rigorous deduplication and strict leakage prevention across temporal walk-forward splits, textual features easily produce illusory gains in volatile time series. Statistical hypothesis testing (Diebold-Mariano) is essential to establish whether textual sentiment adds verifiable predictive power over financial baseline signals.
Repository: github.com/sultanofficial717/brazil-energy

DSPD: A Device-Bound Stateless Password Derivation Scheme

Accepted • FIT 2026
23rd International Conference on Frontiers of Information Technology (FIT 2026), Islamabad
Problem Deriving strong, distinct cryptographic credentials without maintaining an attack-vulnerable central vault or stateful credential synchronization database.
What I did Constructed a derivation scheme using HKDF-SHA256 + Argon2id + hardware-resident device secret, incorporating per-application version counters for rotation; benchmarked derivation latency across five hardware environments.
Result Deterministic credential derivation executed in under 220 ms across five heterogeneous hardware environments, resilient against offline brute-force attacks without central storage.
Reviewers Note
What reviewers asked for: Clearer backup and recovery mechanisms, along with more careful and qualified threat model claims.
What I learned Secure cryptographic design must balance memory hardness against device limits. In addition, backup and disaster recovery procedures require the same formal threat modeling as primary derivation paths.

AutismLens: Multimodal RAG for Autism Screening Interpretation

In Development
Final Year Project • In active development
Problem Clinical interpretation of paper-based autism screening forms is labor-intensive and prone to manual transcription mistakes, requiring verifiable grounding against official diagnostic scoring guidelines.
What I did OCR and OpenCV detect filled marks to extract item-level scores. Then, Sentence-BERT and ChromaDB retrieve exact passages from the official manual so clinicians can verify report citations.
Result Functional end-to-end prototype extracting item-level assessment marks and producing diagnostic summary drafts with direct provenance citations back to verified manual sections.
What I learned Clinical decision support tools must never operate as generative black boxes. Explicit citation and traceable manual provenance are foundational for practitioner trust and verification.
